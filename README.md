# Forever — Party Quest Log

Open the [published guide](https://caidsweet.github.io/Lvlingforever/). For local development, run `python -m http.server 8080 --directory /workspace/Lvlingforever` and visit `http://localhost:8080/`. The static site needs no package installation or backend; recipes load from a local JSON file when their tab opens.

## Classic Interface

The interface uses original Classic interface textures for the stone panels, silver frame corners, quest-book emblem, red buttons, selection highlights, parchment and scroll arrows. The quest view places its list in the left panel and its text/rewards on the right page. Dungeon preparation is an expandable section. The window has working close/reopen controls, independent desktop scrolling and a responsive mobile layout. Progress, quest maps and cached item tooltips remain functional. Friz Quadrata replaces the earlier approximate typefaces; gold UI text is subdued and parchment text is darker. Item rarity/effect colours are preserved.

Edit `assets/ui/classic.css` to change the theme. `scripts/build_site.py` generates `assets/ui/site-classic.css` with relative paths to cached game textures and fonts. The published page starts at the normal 1–10 route. Asset sources and font licences are recorded in `data/ui-assets.json` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

Alliance, 1 tank, 1 healer and 3 DPS. Follow RestedXP for levels 1–10, then a general levelling route to **30**. Twelve dungeon views, 67 quest entries and 108 reward cards remain available, including class quests and follow-ups. Three additional Void Pearl entries are retained as unresolved references because their Forever item records are unavailable. The same quest may appear in multiple dungeons. Ragefire Chasm is excluded. Dalaran and Razorfen Kraul are optional late-20s plans with no verified full-run recommendation before 30. Gnomeregan and Scarlet Monastery notes are saved separately for later planning; quests with a minimum level above 30 are explicitly labelled.

The main route is based on the user's research compiled October 10, 2026, prioritising [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling) and [WoW Forever Guides](https://wowforeverguides.com/leveling). Research & Sources includes all six guide references, their assessed uses and limitations, class-guide links and the supplied October 8 Blizzard patch summary. The linked articles and official post could not be accessed from this runtime (proxy HTTP 403), so these summaries are attributed to the supplied research, rather than claimed as independently verified. The assessment is editable in `data/leveling-research.json` and embedded by the builder.

Zone bands are overlapping alternatives: racial starters, Westfall/Loch Modan/Darkshore around 10–20, then Ashenvale/Wetlands/Duskwood around 20–30, with Redridge to fill gaps. The page does not ask the party to finish every zone. Preparation details are drawn separately from the quest snapshots. No XP-per-hour or fixed completion-time claims are used. The reported approximately 20% dungeon-enemy XP increase is not presented as 20% more total run XP or proof that dungeon farming is faster.

The northern route combines Ruins of Lordaeron at around level 22 with Shadowfang Keep during the same 22–24 trip. Blackfathom Deeps follows at 24–26. Prepare paladin and warlock quests before travelling north, and group the turn-ins after both northern dungeons.

Quest completion and checked prerequisites are saved locally in your browser. In-game quest and item names retain their English names. Item tooltips support mouse hover, keyboard focus and touch. Mouse clicks do not pin them: leaving the reward hides the tooltip immediately. Touch taps toggle a single pinned tooltip; tapping outside, scrolling or pressing Escape dismisses it. All 108 reward cards use cached Wowhead Forever tooltip markup and original icons, covering 106 distinct items fetched on October 10, 2026. The classic dark tooltip frame preserves rarity colours, item level, equipment slots, class requirements, Equip/Use effects, cooldowns and sell-price coins. It follows the cursor and stays within the viewport. Tooltip HTML is filtered before display. Internal stat names and duplicated school-damage totals are excluded from fallback formatting.

Use **Refresh Wowhead Tooltips** in Research & Sources to request live updates from `nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/item/{id}`. A failed request retains the cached successful tooltip and local icon. `python scripts/fetch_items.py` downloads missing tooltips and icons; add `--refresh` to update successful cached records. Failed refreshes preserve prior data. Run `python scripts/build_data.py` afterwards to rebuild the site from the cache. The three unavailable Void Pearl IDs are documented in `data/item-data-gaps.json`; their old names are not displayed as confirmed reward cards.

## Data Sources and Limitations

- [Wowhead Forever](https://www.wowhead.com/forever): primary source for current item tooltips. Direct access succeeded during map research on October 10, 2026. Map positions were extracted from Forever quest pages and cached; later requests returned HTTP 403, so successful research records are preserved rather than replaced by failed refreshes. The browser map workflow was tested using cached images and coordinates.
- [Dungeon Questie](https://github.com/coffeelover1010/dungeon-questie): beta catalogue and recursive prerequisites. Exact snapshot commits are recorded in `data/research-snapshot.json`.
- [GearQuest Forever](https://github.com/Henrik8210/gear-quest-forever): older item stats retained only as a fallback for new imported/missing records. Current reward tooltips use the cached Forever data instead; matching names in older Classic/TBC data do not establish current Forever stats.
- The user’s screenshots confirm quest lists for the seven dungeons shown.

Known gaps are flagged on the page: the Abominable Creatures pickup, some Ruins of Lordaeron quest-starting items, certain Excavation chains, Past Due and the prerequisite for Researching the Corruption. The Treaty of Understanding: the screenshot shows minimum level 9, while the catalogue lists 16. The Fury Runs Deep: the screenshot shows 25, while the catalogue lists 22; the guide plans pickup at 25. City of Dalaran was open in beta according to the catalogue; launch availability and the recommended group level have not been verified live.

All rewards are listed when present in the catalogue. An empty reward list means item rewards are not documented, rather than proving that the quest has none. Choice and guaranteed rewards are not always distinguished in the source. No missing stats or locations have been invented.

## Update the Data

`python scripts/build_data.py` reads the editable snapshots and `assets/ui/guide-template.html`, builds `data/guide.json` and `data/recipe-catalogue.json`, and writes a fully embedded offline copy to `.pages-build/standalone.html`. It then calls `scripts/build_site.py` to generate the small `index.html`, `data/site-recipes.json` and `assets/ui/site-classic.css`. The published site serves all cached icons, maps, textures and fonts from its own assets directory. No packages or external checkouts are needed for this Python build. Generated text and interface copy are in English. `python scripts/build_preview.py` produces the smaller versioned attachment preview, with delayed compressed recipe data and original source image URLs. Versioned `forever-guide-*.html` and screenshot review copies are ignored by Git.

With Playwright available, run `node scripts/check_site.cjs http://localhost:8080/` to decode every icon/map in Chromium and check recipe counts, ranks, source filters, tooltip dismissal, navigation and responsive layout while blocking third-party requests.

## Quest Map

Use **Quest Map** for the full map view, or **Show Quest on Map** inside a quest. Filter by dungeon, quest and zone. Gold `!` markers show quest pickups; gold `?` markers show turn-ins. Hover, focus or tap a marker to see the NPC, quest name, coordinates and prerequisite step. Multiple quests at the same NPC are grouped. Pickups and turn-ins at the same location have separate markers. Prerequisite and follow-up quests are included by default; completed steps can be hidden.

Twenty original WoW Forever / Classic+ in-game zone maps are served locally from `assets/maps/`. Their source is `wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/classicplus/maps/enus/original/{zoneId}.jpg`. The Classic+ path is required: the general Retail path uses different layouts and cannot be used with these coordinates. Coordinates come from the corresponding Wowhead Forever quest mapper data, not guessed positions or converted Retail pins. Horde class trainers in shared quest records are omitted for this Alliance route.

Where Wowhead publishes no world-map position, the location instructions are listed below the map without a marker. Item-started quests and interior pickups are not assigned invented outdoor coordinates. Published coordinates are database evidence, not live-client verification of launch availability.

`python scripts/fetch_maps.py` reads cached successful quest records, downloads map assets, and writes `data/map-snapshot.json`. Add `--refresh` to explicitly refresh quest pages. Failed quest refreshes retain the previous successful record. Run `python scripts/build_data.py` afterwards to rebuild the site with updated coordinates and cached map paths. The cached research records and original downloaded maps are included in `data/wowhead/` and `assets/maps/`.

[Mobalytics — Zone Map Level Ranges](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/guides/zone-map-level-ranges) is a user-recommended planning reference. Its domain was added to the saved network configuration draft, preserving the existing Wowhead domains. Access remains blocked in the current runtime; the article has not been reviewed or used as verified route data. Review and save the network settings and publish the environment to enable the saved configuration.

## Professions

The 1–10 route starts with learning Skinning plus Herbalism or Mining in the starting zone. Local profession trainers sell gathering tools for 10 copper each and materials bags for 25 copper each, following the party’s information supplied October 11, 2026. The setup checklist saves locally and does not count toward completed dungeon quests. Edit `data/professions.json` to update this content.

The Professions tab includes all 12 professions and 2,492 client recipe records from [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/professions), checked against [Wowhead Forever](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/). The trade-skill window includes required skill beside each recipe, original game icons, reagent quantities, output, tools/stations, acquisition categories, recipe-item links, search and source filters. Dropped recipe items are included; a recipe item with an unpublished acquisition source is not assumed to drop. Mark Learned is a local checklist, independent of completed quests.

Required skill uses the Wowhead Forever learn-at field. ForeverDB displays its client `req` value as an orange skill-up threshold; this is not assumed to be a learning requirement. The client skill-up start differs from Wowhead learn-at for 385 records, and both fields are displayed in those recipe details. There are 37 recipes without a published learn-at requirement, shown as Not listed. Skill-up thresholds are separate and labelled as Wowhead data. Trainer/vendor data from Classic is labelled as a reference; observed Forever trainers are identified separately. Client-only records and unavailable recipe items remain in the full catalogue with their status shown. This is a client catalogue across all skills, not a claim that every record is obtainable during launch or before level 30.

The Camping tab includes 38 Alliance camp features across all professions, their notable buffs, detailed scaling and class-buff exclusions, materials and links to the corresponding crafting recipe. Cooking campfires support 3, 5 or 10 additional features. Party-supplied summaries are retained in `data/camping.json`; detailed effects, tier numbers and recipe relationships come from the client snapshot. Skill on a camp card follows its recipe requirement; differing overview values are explained in the expandable details. Iron Oven is included as an additional client feature. All camping-item tooltips and original icons are cached and served locally. Other recipe outputs/materials load current item tooltips on hover when online; unavailable details are explicitly labelled.

Editable source snapshots are in `data/recipe-research/foreverdb/` and `data/recipe-research/wowhead/`. `scripts/build_recipes.py` generates `data/recipe-catalogue.json`. `assets/ui/guide-template.html`, `assets/ui/professions.js` and `assets/ui/classic.css` contain the editable interface, compiled by `python scripts/build_data.py`. `python scripts/fetch_recipes.py` caches missing snapshots and icons; it does not refresh existing successful snapshots. `python scripts/fetch_items.py` also includes the camping items.

Profession rank panels show the skill cap, character-level requirement and skill needed to learn the next rank separately. These use the editable Classic reference in `data/profession-ranks.json`; Forever rank-training gates are explicitly unverified. Production professions show character levels 5/10/20/35 and learning skill 0/50/125/200. Gathering shows levels 1/1/10/25 with the same skill gates; Apprentice and Journeyman have no character-level gate in the linked Classic overview. Fishing uses the actual Expert book requirement (level 20, skill 125) and the Artisan quest gate (level 35, skill 225), which differ from the overview's generic Fishing row. Cooking and First Aid keep their separate book/quest reference profiles. Expand the rank reference below the blue panel for training methods and source links. A skill cap of 75 does not mean you need to wait until 75 to learn Journeyman at skill 50.
